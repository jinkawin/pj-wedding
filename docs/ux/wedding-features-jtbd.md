# Jobs-to-be-Done (JTBD) Analysis: Wedding Guest Experience

## Feature 1: Interactive Seat Map & Guest Finder

### Target Users & Personas
- **Primary Persona**: Wedding Guest (Friend, Relative, Colleague, Family Member).
- **Device Context**: 85% mobile (smartphones) while walking into the ballroom or reception hall, 15% desktop beforehand.
- **Emotional State**: Hurried, festive, slightly stressed about finding where they belong among 200+ guests.
- **Accessibility Needs**: High-contrast labels, legible table numbers, zoom/pan touch controls, instant search feedback.

### Job Statement
> **When** I arrive at the ballroom or plan my arrival,
> **I want to** quickly search my name or table number to visually locate my assigned seat on an interactive floor plan,
> **So I can** walk directly to my table with confidence, without awkward disorientation or crowding the registration desk.

### Incumbent Solution & Pain Points
- **Current**: Traditional printed easel boards with alphabetized lists or printed floor maps.
- **Pain Points**:
  - People cluster around the board, blocking everyone else.
  - Font size is often tiny and hard to read in dim ballroom lighting.
  - Guests can't easily correlate their table number (e.g. Table 8) with where it physically sits relative to the stage, entrance, or restrooms.
- **Opportunity**: Mobile-first instant search with autocomplete, dynamic visual highlight on the floor plan, table guest list breakdown, and stage/entrance reference anchors.

## Feature 2: Wedding Agenda & Event Timeline

### Target Users & Personas
- **Primary Persona**: All invited guests, bridal party, family members.
- **Device Context**: Mobile devices during the event, checking times and flow.
- **Emotional State**: Eager not to miss key moments (entrance, champagne toast, vows, bouquet toss).

### Job Statement
> **When** I am attending the wedding day ceremonies and celebration,
> **I want to** check the schedule of events, times, locations, and dress cues in one clean timeline,
> **So I can** plan my arrival, be seated on time for emotional highlights, and enjoy the party without asking staff or the hosts.

### Incumbent Solution & Pain Points
- **Current**: Physical folded paper programs or word-of-mouth.
- **Pain Points**:
  - Paper programs get left in cars or misplaced.
  - Hard to tell what stage the event is currently in.
- **Opportunity**: Visual timeline with active time indicator, icons for each milestone, elegant typography, venue cues, and dual language support.

## Feature 3: Curated Couple Gallery & Photo Showcase

### Target Users & Personas
- **Primary Persona**: Friends, relatives, overseas guests eager to view memories.
- **Device Context**: Both desktop and mobile, relaxed browsing mode.
- **Emotional State**: Sentimental, joyful, celebratory.

### Job Statement
> **When** I visit the wedding website before or during the wedding,
> **I want to** browse high-resolution photographs of Parima & Jinkawin (pre-wedding, proposal, travel),
> **So I can** celebrate their love story visually and feel deeper connection to their journey.

### Incumbent Solution & Pain Points
- **Current**: Social media albums or slide shows only shown during dinner.
- **Pain Points**:
  - Cluttered UI, slow loading, compression artifacts, uncurated dumps.
- **Opportunity**: Editorial magazine-style grid with category filter tabs (All, Pre-wedding, Proposal, Journey), smooth modal lightbox with keyboard/swipe navigation, caption stories, and fast responsive images.
