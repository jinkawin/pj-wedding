# User Journey: Wedding Guest Navigation & Celebration

## User Persona
- **Who**: Wedding Guest (Friend / Colleague / Extended Family)
- **Goal**: Find assigned table easily, stay informed on the schedule of ceremonies, view couple photos, and enjoy the day.
- **Context**: Arriving at Grand Ballroom in formal attire with phone in hand, or checking the website ahead of time.
- **Success Metric**: Finds assigned seat in < 15 seconds without asking event staff; checks schedule effortlessly; enjoys photo gallery.

---

## Journey 1: Finding My Seat (Seat Map)

### Stage 1: Arrival & Uncertainty
- **Doing**: Walking into the reception foyer from parking/lobby.
- **Thinking**: "Where is table 12? Or wait, which table am I assigned to again?"
- **Feeling**: 😅 Rushed, slight social anxiety, holding phone and invitation envelope.
- **Pain Points**:
  - Crowds blocked at paper easel board.
  - Can't remember if seat was under first name, nickname, or English transliteration.
- **Opportunity**: Mobile navigation button "Seat Map" in navbar, leading directly to a prominent search bar.

### Stage 2: Searching Name
- **Doing**: Taps "Seat Map", types "Priw" or "John" or "Prasert".
- **Thinking**: "Will it match my Thai or English spelling?"
- **Feeling**: 🧐 Focused, eager for instant feedback.
- **Pain Points**:
  - Exact character match failures in traditional systems.
- **Opportunity**: Case-insensitive and partial name search that auto-suggests matches with table name/number and assigned seat.

### Stage 3: Visual Location on Map
- **Doing**: Selecting their name in the results.
- **Thinking**: "Ah, Table 4, Seat 2! Where is Table 4 relative to the entrance and stage?"
- **Feeling**: 💡 Relieved, confident.
- **Pain Points**:
  - Abstract tables with no orientation markers.
- **Opportunity**: Floor plan highlights Table 4 in warm terracotta gold with pulsing indicator; shows "Main Stage" at top and "Grand Entrance" at bottom; displays fellow table guests.

### Stage 4: Seated & Connected
- **Doing**: Walks straight to Table 4, finds their place card, greets friends.
- **Thinking**: "That was seamless! Let me see who else is at our table."
- **Feeling**: 😊 Relaxed, impressed by the modern experience.

---

## Journey 2: Following the Agenda

### Stage 1: Checking Timeline
- **Doing**: While chatting at the cocktail reception, taps "Agenda" on the website.
- **Thinking**: "What time does the formal dinner start? When is the bouquet toss?"
- **Feeling**: 🥂 Festive, curious.
- **Opportunity**: Clear vertical timeline with status badges (Upcoming, In Progress, Completed), ceremony times, venues, and activity icons.

---

## Journey 3: Browsing the Gallery

### Stage 1: Emotional Immersion
- **Doing**: Waiting between dinner courses, opens "Gallery".
- **Thinking**: "I wonder how their proposal in Chiang Mai went and what their pre-wedding shoot looks like!"
- **Feeling**: ❤️ Warm, sentimental.
- **Opportunity**: Clean curated masonry layout, filter by "All", "Pre-Wedding", "Proposal", "Moments", full-screen lightbox modal with captions.
